Our Vision

Our Vision

Our vision is to raise a community of believers with deep spiritual knowledge for salvation, transformed happy living, all round success, and kingdom expansion.

Why EBM

  • ✦ We are a non-denominational church embracing every tribe and culture
  • ✦ We are devoted to deep spiritual growth
  • ✦ We are committed to everyone's transformation and personal growth
  • ✦ We have a huge attention for children and young adults
  • ✦ We have listening ears

Life at EBM

Community & Outreach

Taking the Gospel to the Streets of Dublin

Beyond our four walls, we carry the love of Christ to the streets — offering free prayer and a listening ear to everyone we meet, regardless of tribe, culture, or background. This is who we are: a church devoted to seeing lives transformed, one conversation at a time.
